2026

Studied inverse estimation of oxygen-plasma reaction-rate coefficients using PyTorch neural networks, investigating input-species selection, model ensembles and noise robustness. Achieved strong predictive performance on noise-free simulation data while identifying sensitivity to noisy inputs as a key limitation. Supervised by Prof. Vasco Guerra and Prof. Rodrigo Ventura.

Open-Source Contributions

Agent-Safety Evaluation: AgentDojo Weighting Analysis

2026

Contributed a merged post-publication research extension to Shortcut Sensitivity in Agent-Safety Evaluations. Investigated the effects of evaluation-unit weighting on AgentDojo lookup metrics using grouped cross-validation, domain holdouts and bootstrap uncertainty analysis. Found that changing the evaluation weighting increased the reported leave-one-domain-out F1 score from 0.495 to 0.537 without changing the underlying predictions.
